## Risk, concurrency and evidence

- Develop test-first cases for receipt/rule/interval boundaries and critical atomic operations: competing confirmation, transfer rollback, stale correction, D099/discontinuation and final authority. Exercise the business path against real persisted state. Unit tests alone do not satisfy these checks.
- Use independent connections with deterministic barriers for last quota slot, same shirt, same player across seasons, concurrent activation/close, revocation, duplicate replay and stale basis. Assert post-commit state from a separate connection. Inject failures before audit/commit and on the second transfer side. Verify rollback and same-key replay without duplicate committed effects. Cover known rolled-back retry and uncertain commit handling.
- Verify HQ-01 on in-scope lookup, with 20 concurrent users and ≥95% ordinary requests within 2 seconds on the D097 reference baseline (4 vCPU, 8 GiB RAM, SSD). Record actual hardware/software and disk capacity. Warm up for one minute, measure for ten minutes, report each in-scope lookup group and measure API and UI separately. Verify the applicable TT-02 lookup viewports of 360 × 800, 768 × 1024 and 1366 × 768. Use a reproducible season with 20 clubs and ≥600 Player Profiles, valid registration composition (30 per club under the declared fixture), and authorized portrait assets. Demonstrate actual portrait management and rendering through the backend and selected storage at hundreds-of-profiles scale. Keep fixture dates and training ages isolated from real season defaults.
- Record the TD-01 usability trial as five representative users × four authorized tasks. At least 80% must find the relevant function within 30 seconds and three navigation actions. Record browser, viewport, declared data, load and measured outcomes. Historical full-system load and BM-07 remain later-owner acceptance.
- Run the accepted PHASE 1/2 regression and D097/D098 quality gates on the candidate revision. Record exact revision, dependency evidence, environment and selected storage, fixtures and source rights, executed commands, pass/fail, defects and reviewer. Preserve existing historical evidence. No status changes follow from this design alone.

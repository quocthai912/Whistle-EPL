## Resolution and updates

For each point, record the question, source page, options/basis, team leader decision and approval date, and affected documents/Phases; change to `RESOLVED` only when an identifiable decision exists. Create an ADR under the [domain conventions](../../docs/agents/domain.md) only for important choices involving real trade-offs and lasting consequences. Do not turn open points into new requirements or modify the PDF yourself; business changes require team leader confirmation and updated traceability.

TT-05/external integration is conditional extensibility, not a mandatory blocker requiring provider selection. Deferring integration must still preserve verification of independent operation. V1 is not yet approved; Phase decomposition and interpreted acceptance criteria also require team leader review and are not automatically settled design.

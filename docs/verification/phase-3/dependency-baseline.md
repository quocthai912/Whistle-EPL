## 3. Accepted Media & Backup Tooling Inspection (T010 / T025 Bounded Adaptation)

Inspected `backend/src/media/media-validation.ts`, `local-media.store.ts`, `media.service.ts`, `media.controller.ts`, `media-cleanup.service.ts`, `orphan-inventory.service.ts`, and `ops/backup/set-format.py`, `backup.sh`, `restore.sh`, `ops/backup/tests/test_set_format.py`.

1. **Reusable Seams Already in Place:**
   - `LocalMediaStore`: stores immutable `<assetId>.webp` bytes under private `MEDIA_ROOT` with `wx`, fsync, SHA-256 verification, `.cleanup-` rollback staging, and orphan inventory. It is fully owner-agnostic and directly supports Player portrait bytes.
   - `media-validation.ts`: validates static PNG/JPEG/WebP, `<= 2 MiB` (`2_097_152` bytes), `<= 2048` px per dimension, rejects APNG/animated WebP/MPF JPEG, and normalizes to WebP via `sharp`.
   - `audit-payload.policy.ts`: `MEDIA_ASSET_STORED`, `MEDIA_ASSET_ATTACHED`, `MEDIA_ASSET_REPLACED`, and `MEDIA_ASSET_DETACHED` already allow `ownerKind` in `['CLUB', 'PLAYER']`!
2. **Bounded Adaptation Assigned to T003 & T010:**
   - **T003 (Schema):** `media_asset.club_id` is currently `NOT NULL` with Club-only triggers (`preserve_media_asset_metadata`, `guard_current_logo_lifecycle`). T003 makes `club_id` nullable, adds nullable `player_id` with `CHECK ((club_id IS NOT NULL) <> (player_id IS NOT NULL))`, adds `UNIQUE (player_id, id)`, composite FK `player(id, current_portrait_id) -> media_asset(player_id, id)`, `guard_current_player_portrait`, and extends `preserve_media_asset_metadata` and `guard_current_logo_lifecycle` to enforce identical immutability and unreferenced-lifecycle protection for Player portraits while keeping all existing Club rows and constraints intact.
   - **T010 (Application & Cleanup):**
     - Generalize `normalizeClubLogo` in `media-validation.ts` into a shared static-image validator supporting domain-appropriate Vietnamese error wording for both logos and portraits.
     - Extend `MediaService.readCurrent` (`GET /api/v1/media/:assetId`) to resolve whether `media_asset` belongs to a `Club` or a `Player`, acquire the matching shared owner lock (`club` or `player`) + `mediaAsset` lock to linearize concurrent read vs replacement/removal, verify current pointer ownership (`club.currentLogoId` or `player.currentPortraitId`), enforce owner-specific read authorization (`requireClubRead` vs `requirePlayerRead` / `publicMediaScope`), and emit `Cache-Control: no-store`.

# ADR 0015 - Managed club logos and current branding

This ADR records decisions and rationale; authoritative current rules are owned by [media policy](../../context/english/design/media.md). Historical open-point notes do not reopen subsequently confirmed policy. The Q87 amendment below supersedes the original server-only/no-cloud storage restriction; all other LOGO-01/D069 rules remain in force.

**Status:** accepted. **Approver:** Team Leader. **Date:** 2026-10-25 (Asia/Bangkok). **Decision:** LOGO-01, full foundation package plus option A; first-version media scope also confirmed.

Club logos are managed club-master data under UC07, displayed consistently with fallback. Store files durably on the server outside the repository/executable area; PostgreSQL stores references/metadata and the backend receives/delivers images. Accept static PNG/JPEG/WebP up to 2 MiB and 2048 pixels per dimension, rejecting SVG/GIF/animation. Validate size, actual type, decode and dimensions; normalize server-side and generate filenames rather than trusting client extension/MIME alone. No cloud, general file platform or image library/version is selected.

[D034](../../context/english/use-cases/administration.md#d034---separately-assigned-shared-master-record-management-permissions) shared club-management authority permits upload/replace/remove to fallback with operator/time/before-after history; [D037](../../context/english/use-cases/administration.md#d037---prohibit-administrator-self-granting-of-shared-master-management-permissions)-[D041](../../context/english/use-cases/administration.md#d041---retain-shared-master-management-assignments-while-an-account-is-locked) session/authority lifecycle and [D030](../../context/english/design/architecture.md#d030---recheck-current-session-and-authority-at-the-final-decision-point-of-writes) final checks apply. Switch references only after successful validation/storage; failure preserves the previous logo. Share aspect-preserving display, club-name alternative text and missing/load-failure fallback. Backup/restore includes both files and metadata.

The Team Leader chose current branding over historical image snapshots: current logos appear even on old-season views, with change logs but no requirement to retain past files for seasonal reconstruction. This explicitly refines [D059](../../context/english/business.md#d059---required-registration-data-and-official-snapshot-milestones) for logos only. Other approved historical names, snapshots, results and statistics remain protected.

The latest instruction also requires a complete 20-club season with logos, hundreds of player profiles and managed portraits, without frontend hardcoding or external-API dependence. Existing smaller [D060](../../context/english/use-cases/scheduling.md#d060---automatic-fixture-pairing-with-manually-completed-schedule-drafts) configurations remain supported. Player club/shirt information stays seasonal; avatar-specific validation/retention/history is not silently approved by LOGO-01. Kit color/clothing-image management is not required.

Current Context documents may be updated under the single-package policy. Preserve the v1 approval commit/PDF and Phase statuses/evidence. This records decisions, not implementation or acceptance. See [media policy](../../context/english/design/media.md) and [integration gate](0016-core-acceptance-before-external-integration.md).

## Q69 - Managed player portraits

**Status:** accepted. **Decision:** D069. The Team Leader selected Q69 option A ("Tôi chọn A."), approving current-image display together with the common portrait package. See [the current owner](../../context/english/design/media.md#d069---managed-player-portraits-with-current-image-display).

At LOGO-01 approval, portrait-specific policy was still open. Q69 now explicitly reuses its storage/validation contract for portraits, under separately granted player-master authority and final session checks, with safe replacement/removal, audit, controlled public display and fallback. It selects current portraits on historical pages instead of registration-snapshot image retention, simplifying first-version storage and display while accepting that a past season may show a newer portrait. This explicitly extends D059's media exception without changing historical registration facts, reports or statistics. The approved common package and technical limits are owned by D069, not inferred from the earlier logo approval. No image library, provider, external integration, implementation or whole-package approval is selected.

## Q87 - Environment-specific media storage amendment

**Status:** accepted. **Decision:** D087 amendment to LOGO-01/D069. **Approver:** Team Leader, 2026-10-26 (Asia/Bangkok). The explicit supplemental approval retains server/persistent-volume media for local/Compose use and permits Supabase Storage as an alternative when deploying to the Internet, without simultaneous storage in both locations. [Current storage policy](../../context/english/design/media.md#environment-specific-media-storage) is the sole rule owner; [ADR 0001](0001-modular-layered-backend.md#q87---docker-compose-reference-deployment) records the associated deployment choice.

The trade-off is deployment flexibility while retaining a simple backend-controlled media boundary, at the cost of provider availability and provider-specific recovery configuration when remote storage is selected. PostgreSQL keeps references/metadata, backend permissions/session/file checks and safe image changes remain intact, credentials remain protected, and public storage cannot bypass authorized delivery. Durability and backup/restore must include actual content as well as metadata for the selected provider. This storage exception does not authorize a football-data API, image library, SDK, Supabase Auth/Database or Vercel/Render, and does not change historical/current-image policy. Exact delivery/backup/cleanup mechanisms remain open. Earlier no-provider statements in this ADR describe the original LOGO-01/Q69 approvals, not the amended current storage options.

## Q90 - Acceptance dataset and demonstration boundary

**Status:** accepted. **Decision:** D090. **Approver:** Team Leader. The Team Leader answered "Tôi chọn A." to Q90, approving the whole proposed package. [D090](../../context/english/quality.md#d090---reproducible-acceptance-datasets-and-season-fixtures) owns the acceptance-data rules; LOGO-01/D069 continue to own managed media behavior.

Choose reproducible controlled fixtures separated from sourced demonstration data rather than making real Premier League data the main basis of all testing. This improves repeatability, boundary-case coverage and independence from external providers, accepting the need to identify provenance and simulation clearly. The full-season baseline is at least 600 Player Profiles with 20 clubs/38 rounds/380 matches, actual managed club logos and portrait functionality at hundreds-of-profiles scale. The package retains 10-season/3,800-match history tests, per-template import/export fixtures of at least 100 valid records, explicit seasonal test parameters, expected outputs and resetability. It does not invent official seasonal values, increase squad limits, require portraits on every save, bypass operational approval or hardcode frontend data. Original quality thresholds and software/Phase approval status remain unchanged. Earlier exact-player-count open notes are historical for this now-settled minimum; fixture contents, authorized media sources and measured environment details remain elaboration.
